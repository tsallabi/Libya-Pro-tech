Local Payments

The Achilles' heel of most Libyan stores: Payment Collection.

A beautiful store without working payment is merely an advertising page. We integrate payment collection as a core system component, not a last-minute add-on.

  1. 01

    Integrate Your Preferred Payment Gateway

    We integrate the bank cards, digital wallets, and local payment services your provider offers, leveraging the APIs and keys they grant you.

  2. 02

    Payment Links for Off-Platform Orders

    Order via WhatsApp or phone? Generate a payment link with a specific amount and time validity, closing the sale without manual bank transfers.

  3. 03

    Controlled Cash on Delivery (COD)

    Enable COD for specific areas and amounts, with daily recording and settlement of agent collections, ensuring no discrepancy between sales and received payments.

  4. 04

    Locked Daily Settlement

    A daily report reconciles orders with receipts, identifies discrepancies and their source, and is locked to prevent retroactive modifications.

  5. 05

    WooCommerce & Existing Stores

    Have an existing store you don't want to rebuild? We integrate payments, inventory, and reporting with your current setup instead of replacing it.

Launch Path

From First Meeting to First Sale

These are realistic timelines for a moderately complex store. Larger projects are divided into successive phases, launched incrementally rather than waiting a full year.

  1. 01

    Week 1: Scope

    A single session defines what will and won't be built, item categorization, and shipping and pricing strategies.

  2. 02

    Week 2: Interfaces

    Approve clickable mockups before development: homepage, product page, cart, and checkout.

  3. 03

    Weeks 3-6: Development

    Bi-weekly releases on your private demo link, allowing you to access, test, and provide feedback directly.

  4. 04

    Week 7: Data & Payments

    Input items, images, and prices; integrate the payment gateway; and conduct real, small-value test purchases.

  5. 05

    Week 8: Launch

    Train your team on the control panel, launch, then receive two weeks of post-launch support to fine-tune based on real-world usage.
