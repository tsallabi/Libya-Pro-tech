The Challenge

What was hindering operations

Most Quran apps use the Hafs recitation, while people in Libya read with the Qalun recitation from Nafi', and they need prayer times for their cities and an app that works offline.

The Solution

What we built

  1. Scanned Mushaf Pages with Qaloon's RecitationIn King Fahd Complex script with touch-selectable verses.
  2. Six Interpretations and Full SearchSearch the full Mushaf text and an index by Surahs and Juz'.
  3. Recited Mushaf and Khatmah PlanLibyan reciters at the top of the list, and daily recitation.
  4. Libyan City Prayer Times and QiblaAstronomical calculation, works offline after first launch.
