The Challenge

What was hindering operations

Libyan buyers from Chinese stores are surprised upon receipt by shipping and customs fees, find no local payment options, no pickup location, and don't know how long their shipment will take.

The Solution

What we built

  1. Final Price in LYDEach product with its final price, including shipping and customs, by air or sea, with estimated duration.
  2. Order by LinkAny product from any Chinese website is priced and purchased on behalf of the customer.
  3. Pickup or DeliveryFree pickup from Tripoli or Benghazi warehouse, or door-to-door delivery with stage-by-stage tracking.
  4. Two Backend DashboardsProduct import and automated translation management, plus a dashboard for the shipping partner in China.
