The Challenge

What was hindering operations

Car import involves purchasing, sea and land shipping, containers, invoices, and multiple branches; each stage was in a different file, and clients called to ask about their car.

The Solution

What we built

  1. Import Cost CalculatorFrom lot number to customer's door.
  2. Ten Stages to DeliveryEach vehicle has a clear status from purchase to delivery.
  3. Customer Container TrackingOpen tracking page without login reduces inquiries.
  4. Multi-Branch SafesA safe for each branch and currency, with invoices including grace periods and penalties.
