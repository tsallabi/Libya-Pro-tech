The Challenge

What was hindering operations

Arabic books need to reach readers directly, with in-browser reading without downloads, paid subscriptions, and a publishing dashboard that doesn't require a developer for every new book.

The Solution

What we built

  1. Book Index and PageBrowse by categories, with a dedicated page for each book offering reading and download options.
  2. In-Browser ReaderThe reader can access the book without downloading the file.
  3. Premium SubscriptionExclusive content for subscribers within the platform.
  4. Publishing DashboardManage books, categories, and subscriptions from a single dashboard.
