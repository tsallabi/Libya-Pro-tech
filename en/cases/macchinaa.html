The Challenge

What was hindering operations

The Libyan buyer navigates US auction sites in English, cannot bid live themselves, and does not know the final cost until the invoice arrives.

The Solution

What we built

  1. Six Auctions on One ScreenCopart, IAAI, ADESA, Manheim, ACV, and OpenLane, updated directly from the auctions.
  2. Shipping-Aware FiltersAuction and price, document availability, and shipping eligibility — before bidding.
  3. Bid with Your Set LimitAutomatic bidding up to the customer's limit, with a car history report in USD and cost to destination.
  4. Full TransparencyDaily auction results, auction calendar, and nine tracking stages until delivery.
