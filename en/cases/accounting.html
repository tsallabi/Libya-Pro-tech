The Challenge

What was hindering operations

Each company in the group had its own accounts and Excel files, and a complete picture of the group only emerged after manual consolidation at month-end, with mixed Dinar and Dollar figures.

The Solution

What we built

  1. Unified Group DashboardProfits, expenses, and cash for all companies on one screen.
  2. Complete Financial StatementsIncome statement, balance sheet, cash flows, trial balance, and a printable Libyan tax report.
  3. Separate CurrenciesDinar and Dollar do not mix in any report.
  4. AI-Powered Invoice ReadingInvoice image from mobile converts to an entry, with import from Excel and Google Sheets.
