The Challenge

What was hindering operations

Car auction ads are all alike: car photos and prices. AutoPro needed content Libyans would remember and share, without the cost of filming actors and locations for each episode.

The Solution

What we built

  1. Two Consistent Characters"Hajj Khalifa" and "Salem" with consistent features in all episodes, recognizable to viewers from the first second.
  2. Libyan Dialect ScriptEveryday dialogue relatable to people, with each episode ending with a single message: Look, More, Go Home.
  3. AI-Powered ProductionGenerated scenes, followed by editing and animation for the auction counter and end card.
  4. Platform-ReadyVertical 9:16, embedded Arabic subtitles, and audio adjusted to ‎-14 LUFS for Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok.
