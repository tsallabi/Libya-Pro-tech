The Challenge

What was hindering operations

Offices in Libya, Egypt, Turkey, and the United States operate with different currencies, and each office has its own vault and commissions. Reconciliation is manual, and the transfer status is unclear to both the customer and the other office.

The Solution

What we built

  1. Precise Transfer JourneyEach transfer goes through named stages, and each office knows its real-time status.
  2. Multi-Currency Vaults and WalletsMain and partner vaults and separate wallets for each role, with deposits and withdrawals.
  3. Commission AccountingCommissions and profits calculated daily, monthly, and annually without external spreadsheets.
  4. Bill Payment and Supplier SettlementAdditional services within the platform itself, replacing external systems.
