The Challenge

What was hindering operations

A new marketing studio needs a name and identity like no other, and a website that turns visitors into WhatsApp conversations instead of just browsing.

The Solution

What we built

  1. A name from the city's history“Oea” is the ancient name of Tripoli, one of the three cities that gave the city its name.
  2. A logo of three shapesA circle for people, three bars for strategy and a triangle for technology — reading OEA.
  3. Colour and type systemInk, limestone and orange, with Alexandria, Archivo and Instrument Serif.
  4. A site that sells through conversation“Send us your page” opens WhatsApp with a ready message and logs the lead in the admin dashboard.
