The Challenge

What was hindering operations

Receivables, collections, and profits were tracked in separate ledgers and spreadsheets, delaying the identification of late-paying customers, and daily inventory reconciliation took a long time before closing the day.

The Solution

What we built

  1. Single Monitoring DashboardReceivables, collections, profits, and supplier balances on a single screen that opens with the shift.
  2. Receipt Vouchers and Daily InventoryEvery amount is entered with a numbered voucher, and the inventory closes the day, revealing any discrepancies immediately.
  3. Automated Delay AlertsThe system tracks late payers and sends alerts via WhatsApp without staff intervention.
  4. Printable Monthly ReportsA ready PDF report for management and partners at the end of each month.
