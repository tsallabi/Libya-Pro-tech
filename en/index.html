    Security & Compliance

    What Risk Management Asks Before Tech Management Does.

    In regulated sectors, a lack of clear security answers leads to immediate disqualification. These are our proactive answers to your critical questions.

    Access Control

    Role and field-level permissions, time-limited sessions, and an immutable audit log for every sensitive operation.

    Data Protection

    Encryption in transit and at rest, strict separation of production and test data, and data anonymization in non-production environments.

    Hosting Location

    Flexible hosting options: on-premise or on a dedicated cloud, with explicit data residency defined in the contract.

    Business Continuity

    Scheduled backups and documented, periodic recovery testing — not merely the existence of a copy.

    Source Code Delivery

    Code, documentation, and database schemas are client-owned, with provisions for third-party review.

    Incident Response

    Defined escalation channels, contracted response times, and root cause analysis reports following every incident.

    Methodology

    Project Flow: From First Meeting to Post-Delivery

    1. 01

      Analysis Session

      One meeting to produce a written scope document: what will be built, what will not, and success metrics.

    2. 02

      Clickable screens you approve before a single line of code is written.

    3. 03

      Phased Development

      Bi-weekly releases on your private demo link, for your direct review and feedback.

    4. 04

      Acceptance Testing

      Your team runs real-world scenarios on live data before launch; we correct issues before delivery.

    5. 05

      Launch and Support

      Documented training, followed by bug fix warranty, then a support contract with defined response times.
