Data Sovereignty

The data is the entity's data, and the system is its system.

  1. 01

    On-Premise Hosting

    Option for full operation on the entity's servers without external connection, or on a dedicated cloud infrastructure whose location is specified in the contract.

  2. 02

    Full Source Code Delivery

    The repository, documentation, and database schemas are owned by the entity and subject to external auditor review. No closed parts, no annual licenses to be extorted with.

  3. 03

    Audit Log for Every Action

    Who changed the transaction status, when, and what the value was before and after the change. The log is undeletable from any interface.

  4. 04

    Training That Ends Reliance on Us

    Video and document-documented training for the entity's team, aiming for you to operate the system independently, not to remain reliant on us.

  5. 05

    Controlled Access for the Technical Team

    No permanent access to production. Access is granted via a documented request for a specific person and duration, the session is recorded, and access is revoked after the task.

Frankly

Why Government Projects Usually Fail, and How We Avoid It

Most failures are not technical. These are four recurring reasons, and our solution for each is written in the contract, not in the presentation.

  1. 01

    Scope That Expands Endlessly

    From the first session, we produce a scope document defining what will and won't be built, along with success metrics. Any subsequent additions go through independent pricing and scheduling.

  2. 02

    One Delivery at the End

    We deliver a release every two weeks via a private link for the entity, allowing managers to see progress firsthand and correct course early.

  3. 03

    Old Data Not Migrated

    Migration starts early, not in the last week, with a reconciliation report for each batch explaining what was entered, what was rejected, and why.

  4. 04

    Untrained Employee Did Not Use

    Acceptance testing is conducted by the entity's employees on real-world workday scenarios, and there's no launch before their signature.
